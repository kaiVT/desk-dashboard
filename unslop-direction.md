# Unslop direction

unslop by Damir Sokolovsky (@heydamir), CC BY 4.0.

## Product
An iPhone home-screen dashboard for glancing at New York time, Reston weather, and a personal US stock watchlist.

## Character
Exact, quiet, instrumental.

## World
A desk instrument at the market close: a clock and a tape of prices under a lamp, after the room has gone still. Surfaces stay graphite. Warmth lives in a small amber session mark and in the typographic clock, not in the chrome.

## Moments
The clock is the one rich element — large, tabular, typographic, like a desk clock face. Up and down in the list use quiet semantic color only on the change figures.

## Signature
A small session lamp next to the time status: a 6px dot that is amber when the US market is open and muted when it is 盘前, 盘后, or 休市. Used only there.
